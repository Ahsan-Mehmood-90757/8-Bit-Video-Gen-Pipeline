# Pipeline V3: Visual-First (no voice, no captions)

V3 removes the TTS narrator and the spoken captions. The video is carried by three things instead:
- **motion**
- **impact micro-text**: 2–5 word pixel badges stuck to the moving parts
- **frame-locked 8-bit audio**: sound effects plus a rhythmic pulse/bass groove, at full volume with no ducking under a voice

v2 is untouched. Its folders (`engine/`, `pipeline/`, `templates/episode`) still work exactly as before, so you can compare v2 and v3 renders side by side. V3 *reuses* the v2 core read-only (pixel engine, camera, cutaways, arrows, HUD) and adds its own layers on top.

```
engine_v3/
  sim_base_v3.html      host page: v2 core + the v3 layers below
  micro_text.js         impact micro-text, hook banner, payoff stamp, beat clock
  safe_zone_v3.js       UI-safe box (90..920 x 260..1480), fitBoundsToSafeZone, per-frame QA tracking
  synth_engine.js       preview audio: master chain, 3-voice cap, C-major pentatonic <= 880 Hz
  audio_events_v3.js    logSfxV3 / logSynthStateV3 / logBeatPulseV3 (v2's R.sfx and R.hum are routed here)
  sfx_presets_v3.js     kick, bass, hat, snare + pop, hop, wrap, warn, stamp, evict, hit, swoosh, banner
pipeline_v3/
  render_v3.py          T timeline → B frames → C mix_v3.wav → E encode  (no TTS, no Whisper, no .ass)
  synth_v3.py           hits + filter-swept continuous voices + beat pulses + groove → lookahead limiter
  new_episode_v3.py     scaffold from templates/episode_v3 (standalone, or inside a channel)
episodes_v3/
  test_ring_buffer_v3/  pipeline test episode (28 s, own pulse groove, music off)
  test_depth_fx_v3/     2.5D / trails / particles / slow-mo / step mode / speed-coupled groove test (16 s)
  binary-vs-linear-search/  Linear vs Binary Search (31 s)
```

- **Beats** are fixed time ranges. There's no narrator to pace them.

## UI-safe zone (mandatory)

Everything must stay inside **x 90..920, y 260..1480** on the 1080×1920 frame. This leaves room for the Shorts/Reels buttons on the right, the header at the top and the title bar at the bottom. Note that the right margin (160 px) is wider than the left (90 px), so V3 centres its banner and stamp on the safe zone, not on the screen.

- **Labels:** micro-text, labels, hook banner, payoff stamp and pointer tags are clamped inside the box, including during their pop and shake animations.
- **Camera view:** it defaults to the safe box, so world drawing is clipped to it. Use `fit` keyframes or `fitBoundsToSafeZone()` so a zoomed-in array is never cut off.
- **Cut-off elements:** world text and boxes that would be cut by the view edge are not drawn at all. Zooms show only whole nodes, and those hidden elements are listed in the report.
- **QA check:** `--preview-only` tracks every text box, box, tag, micro-text and `R.node()` on **every frame**. If anything extends outside the box, QA **fails** (exit code 2), the offenders are outlined in magenta on the QA PNGs, and `qa/safe_zone_report.json` lists them with time, position and overflow. A faint magenta dashed border shows the box on the QA PNGs only; final renders never draw it.

## Attention rules (enforced by `--preview-only` QA)

1. **Reactive text only.**
   - Every text element is at most 4 words and appears on a specific event.
   - It pops in at scale 0.7 → 1.05 → 1.0 over 120 ms (`R.popScale`, automatic for `R.micro`, `R.hookBanner` and `R.payoffStamp`), then **clears** (`dur`) or **dims** (`dimAfter`, alpha 0.4).
   - QA **fails** any bright text (alpha ≥ 0.6) that stays unchanged on screen for more than 5 s, and any text over 4 words.
   - Text that moves or re-pops (a pointer tag following each hop) restarts its 5-second clock.
   - Values like array items and counts are data, not labels. Draw them with `R.digits()` or `R.text(..., {data: true})` and they're exempt.
2. **Open with tension.** Frames 0.0–2.2 s are a high-contrast visual hook plus a 3–4 word question or contrast (`R.hookBanner('WHICH FINDS\n72 FIRST?', {dur: 2.2})`).
3. **Guide the eye.** On every action, frame the active zone with `Camera.track({fit: [...]})` and call `R.spotlight(x, y, w, h)`. Everything outside the zone is dimmed and desaturated. The spotlight glides between zones and fades out when you stop calling it, and text pops stay bright above it.
4. **No orphan fragments.** World text, boxes, digits, brackets and glows that would be cut by the camera view are not drawn at all.

## Density, depth and pacing (`engine_v3/depth_fx_v3.js`)

| Call | What it does |
|---|---|
| `R.setTimeScale(scale, ramp)` | Eased change of simulation speed: `1.0` is real time, `0.15` is slow-mo. Use `R.simDt` / `R.simTime` to move things, so they slow down too. |
| `R.setStepMode(on)` | Turns STEP MODE on or off (discrete stepping) |
| `R.wall25d(x, y, w, h, {depth, dir, face, top, side, hatch})` | Extruded, bevelled cutaway wall: drop shadow, lit top, dark side, hatched cut face and a highlight edge |
| `R.trail(id, x, y, {n, color, size})` | The last N positions of a fast part at decaying alpha. Call it every frame. |
| `R.emit(n, {...})` / `R.stream(id, {rate, ...})` | Pooled particles (900): `spark`, `dot`, `packet` or `ember`, with velocity, drag, gravity and lifetime. They move with simulation time. |
| `R.flowDots(points, {speed, spacing})` | Dots streaming along a path (slow-mo aware) |
| `R.speedBadge({x, y, from})` | REAL SPEED / SLOW-MO ×0.15 / STEP MODE. Pops when the mode changes, then dims. |

**Anti-dead-air rule (QA-enforced).**
- While frames render, the pacing analyser measures, inside the safe box, the pixels that changed from the previous frame and the share of the box that holds content.
- **Dead air** is more than 1.2 s with fewer than 40 changed pixels per frame. An **empty void** is more than 1.2 s with less than 8% of the safe box showing anything. Either one fails `--preview-only`, with the time ranges listed in `qa/pacing_report.json`.
- Fixes: add a micro-event (a particle stream, pointer step, bar tick or callout), accelerate or zoom into the remaining side, keep the camera framed tight, or end the scene sooner.

## Continuous music bed (on by default)

- **What it is:** bass + soft kick + offbeat hat + pulse stab on a C-major-pentatonic progression, warmed by a 600 Hz low-pass, with fade in and out. Settings: `"music": {"bpm", "level", "progression", "duck"}`. Use `"music": false` to turn it off.
- **Speed-coupled.** The groove's clock integrates the logged time scale:
  - in slow-mo the tempo eases down (×0.15 gives 0.575× tempo)
  - the pitch drops like a slowing tape (up to −7 semitones; ×0.15 gives about −6, verified C2 65 Hz → 46 Hz)
  - impact envelopes stretch (×0.15 gives 2× longer decays) instead of leaving silence
- **Ducking:** about −5 dB under major hits; small ticks barely duck it.
- **Preview:** the browser preview plays the same groove (`synth_engine.js`), following `R.setTimeScale` live.

## Audio hygiene (engine-enforced, preview and render)

- **No stray background sound.** The music bed is the only continuous layer (and it's optional). Continuous voices (`R.synth`) only sound while an episode explicitly drives them, and the preview stops their oscillators when idle.
- **Clean envelopes on every layer:** an 8–15 ms attack from 0.0001, a release of at least 15 ms falling exponentially to 0.0001, then true digital zero.
- **Noise only as an impact:** a single short hit, at most 0.1 s including its release.
- **No stacking:** the same sound can't re-trigger within 75 ms, and the 3-voice cap per bus still applies.
- **Verified in every QA and full render.** The audio report measures the noise floor in silent gaps (must be ≤ −60 dBFS):
  - with the music bed on, it checks the separate SFX stem (`mix_v3_sfxstem.wav`)
  - without it, it checks the mix and the final MP4

## Musical audio mastering

The live preview (`engine_v3/synth_engine.js`, Web Audio) and the final MP4 soundtrack (`pipeline_v3/synth_v3.py`) apply the same rules:

1. **Signal chain:** `voiceGain (max 0.16)` → `lowpass 2000 Hz, Q 0.8` → `compressor (threshold −18 dB, knee 12, ratio 12, attack 3 ms, release 150 ms)` → `masterGain 0.75`.
2. **Polyphony:** a strict 3-voice cap per bus (sound-effects bus and rhythm bus). When a 4th voice starts, the oldest fades out in 15 ms. Rhythm has its own bus so effects never cut the beat.
3. **Notes:** every pitched sound is snapped to **C major pentatonic** and shifted down by whole octaves until its highest note is ≤ 880 Hz. Arpeggios keep their shape. Square and saw waves become warm triangles (≥ 200 Hz) or low filtered pulses. Noise percussion is unchanged.
4. **Final loudness (MP4 only):** the chain above is deliberately quiet (the test mix came out at −33.6 LUFS). Stage C applies one clean gain to −14 LUFS, then the lookahead limiter holds peaks at −1 dBFS. This avoids FFmpeg's pumping "dynamic" loudness mode.

To customise, add an `"audio"` block to `script.json`, e.g. `{"musical": false}` to keep raw 8-bit pitches, `{"polyphony": 4}`, `{"lufs": -16}`, or `{"lowpassHz": 3000}`.

**Verified on `test_ring_buffer_v3`:**
- highest note 880 Hz
- 0.23% of energy above 2.5 kHz
- hits within 2 ms of their frame (filter delay plus a 2 ms attack, well inside one 16.7 ms frame)
- final MP4 at −14.0 LUFS with peaks at −0.9 dBFS
